# Payment, split and refund rules

All money is integer VND, bounded to 1,000,000,000 per order/payment. The order total is the sum of the immutable unit-price snapshots for non-cancelled items. Taxes, discounts and service fees are not currently part of the product model or accepted API, so there is no tax/discount value to allocate across split portions. If introduced, those fields need explicit order snapshots and an integer largest-remainder allocation rule before use.

An order may have many payment records. A payment is `PENDING`, `SETTLED` or `FAILED`; only settled rows count as collected revenue. New cash payments settle immediately and require the acting cashier's open shift. `amount` is the portion applied to the order; `tendered` and `change` are recorded per cash tender, but the shift counts `amount` once. A bank transfer requires a unique external reference and remains pending until an owner/manager verifies it or records a reasoned failure. Pending amounts reserve balance and prevent overpayment, but are excluded from sales and reports. Failed/cancelled attempts are retained as status history and do not reduce the balance. There is no automatic expiry for stale pending transfers; managers can leave them unverified while the order remains locked against item changes.

Card checkout is production-disabled. `LabeledCardSandboxAdapter` is explicitly `SANDBOX_ONLY`, has no acquiring credentials and always rejects authorization; it never reports a successful card transaction. Connect an authorized provider and implement signed callback verification, provider idempotency and settlement/refund reconciliation before enabling card payments.

Bill portions are append-only records linked to an existing order. They contain either an explicit VND amount or selected immutable order-item IDs and never create tickets, order items or stock reservations. Each payment can link to one portion; cumulative payment allocation cannot exceed the portion amount, while the order-level payment lock prevents collection above the remaining total. Integer weighted allocations use deterministic largest remainders (`allocateVnd`) for integrations that need to apportion future shared totals.

Refunds are separate append-only requests linked to the original settled payment and optionally an order item. Cashier/manager roles may request; owner/manager must decide with a reason. Approved cash refunds append a `REFUND` movement to an open shift of the approving cashier in the same transaction; closed shift history is never changed. The original payment is immutable. Cumulative requested/approved refunds reserve refundable balance, item refunds are bounded by the item's snapshot price, and legacy direct cash-refund movements count toward the same cap. Refunds do not restore consumed ingredients. Non-cash disbursement is manually performed outside OMNIHUB and approval is not a provider settlement callback.

Order payment state is `UNPAID → PARTIALLY_PAID → PAID`, with `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED` and `REFUNDED` after approved refunds. Order completion occurs only when settled payments cover the due total. Pending payments block item mutation. A prepayment whole-order cancellation is atomic: reserved ingredients are released, consumed ingredients are recorded as waste, and no payment is created. Cancellation is rejected if any payment is pending or settled; refunds never restock ingredients. There is no whole-order cancellation-after-payment workflow.

Every financial command uses the store-scoped serializable command bus, a UUID idempotency key, authorization revalidation, an immutable audit row and an outbox event. Order row locks serialize competing tender attempts; PostgreSQL unique constraints protect command keys, external references and refund movements. Payment/refund rows are immutable except the single allowed `PENDING → SETTLED/FAILED` payment transition and `REQUESTED → APPROVED/REJECTED` refund decision. Duplicate verification cannot settle twice. All list/history APIs scope by tenant and store.

Business date is assigned from server transaction time using `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` and the store's 04:00 cutoff. Payment/refund business dates are immutable. Daily reports show settled gross payments, approved refunds and net amount by business date. These reports are operational summaries, not tax filings, bank settlement statements or audited accounting exports.
